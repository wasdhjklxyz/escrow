An agent changed this system. Who asked, who checked, who approved — and can you prove it?

The agent runs in a jail and its changes come out as a proposal. Three different people request, review and sign it, each with an SSH signature. Apply is atomic, and every step goes into a hash-chained log you can verify offline.
