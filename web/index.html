An agent changed this system. Who asked, who checked, who approved, and can you prove it?

The agent runs in a jail. Its changes come out as a proposal that three different people request, review and sign, each with an SSH key. Apply is atomic, and every step goes into a hash-chained log anyone can verify offline.

Acting as

Demo personas: their keys live on this server so you can play every role. In production each key stays with its person. Struck-through buttons are ones this persona may not use (wrong role, or its own proposal). Press one anyway: escrow itself refuses.
